You are compiling a list of proper names for a study of how names are built. Work carefully; the list will be checked against dictionaries.

Task: compile about 1,400 distinct proper names of Brahui (between 1,260 and 1,540), of every kind: people, tribes, clans and sections, lineages and families, saints, shrines and other holy beings, places, rivers, hills and mountains.

Sources, in this order:
1. Proper names recorded for Brahui speakers and the Brahui country in Denys Bray, The Brahui Language (1909; part 2, 1934), the Baluchistan District Gazetteers (1906–07, especially Kalat, Sarawan and Jhalawan), and the Census of India 1901 and 1911 Baluchistan tribal tables. Mark these source "colonial".
2. Only if step 1 gives fewer than about 1,400: add names from later Brahui scholarship (Josef Elfenbein, Mikhail Andronov). Mark these source "later".
You may use the web to consult these works and Brahui dictionaries. Include only names you are confident are real and attested. Do not invent names and do not pad the list. If you cannot reach 1,260 confident names, stop where you are and say so.

For each name give:
- "name": the name in Roman transliteration as in Bray or Elfenbein (ā ī ū ē ō, ṭ ḍ ṛ ṇ ḷ, x for the velar fricative, ɣ or gh for its voiced pair), stem form.
- "type": one of person, group, lineage, deity, place, river, hill, celestial, title, other.
- "morphemes": the name split into its meaningful members, each in stem form, same transliteration, lowercase. A compound name gives its members. A simple name gives one member. Drop patronymic and descent-marking suffixes and the vowel strengthening that goes with them: give the base name; if the base name is already in the list as its own entry, leave the patronymic form out. Do not split a name whose analysis is unknown or disputed; give it whole.
- "gloss": a few English words for the meaning of each member, in order, separated by " + " (for example "black + hill"). Use "name" for a member with no known meaning.
- "source": "colonial" or "later".
