# Umm al-Quwain "Indus-sign seal" (board task #16, M1, agent H4)

## Verdict: untestable. There is no primary report, so the object stays out of the foreign corpus.

The only source is the news-aggregator item flagged by the research sweep (ittihaas.com, about April 2026). It describes a round softstone seal from "a site in Umm al-Quwain" with Indus signs, a humped bull and a date of c. 2300 BCE. That page now returns HTTP 404, and archive.org could not be fetched from here. I found:
- no report from the UAQ Department of Tourism and Archaeology or WAM;
- no report in The National, Khaleej Times or Gulf News, in English or Arabic;
- nothing from the Italian Archaeological Mission at Tell Abraq, and no 2025-26 journal article;
- no photo, no findspot and no sign sequence.

The wording ("circular softstone/steatite seal, Indus script, humped bull") is the stock description of a Gulf Type seal (Laursen 2010; British Museum catalogue entries). That fits a rewritten or generated news item at least as well as a new find.

The one scholarly Umm al-Quwain seal is Majchrzak & Degli Esposti 2024, *PAM* 33:229-243 (open access; PDF in `raw/`). It is a domed Dilmun-related stamp seal, TAD.0105, from the Abraq 2 necropolis: diameter 17.4 mm, two opposed horned animals, Late Bronze to Early Iron Age context. It has no Indus signs and no humped bull, so it is not the reported object.

## What would make it usable
A primary report with an image legible enough to read the signs (excavator paper, UAQ/WAM release, AAE or PAM article). Until then the row in `candidate_objects.csv` is graded EXCLUDE. It must not enter the crib attack (#4), because a sign sequence of unknown reading would only add noise.
