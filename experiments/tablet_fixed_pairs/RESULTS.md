# Do later-published tablets carry the same fixed numeral pairs? (prereg `tablet_fixed_pairs`, hashed 2026-11-23)

**Verdict: untestable.**
- Only 5 numeral tokens on the five sealed heads fall on tablets outside M77, against a minimum of 10. They match the sealed value 2 times out of 5 (rate 0.40).
- ICIT-only seals keep their frozen M77 modal pairs at a similar rate: 15 of 34 (0.44). This is descriptive, not a test.
- **Copper (secondary, not tested):** 6 of 6 match. ICIT and M77 copper tablets are largely the same Mohenjo-daro objects, so this is not out of sample.

## What this means
- The exploratory note in `numerals_by_object` (tablet numerals come in fixed pairs) stays exploratory.
- Out of sample, the five pairs barely occur at all. The 396 later-catalogued Harappa tablet legends carry almost none of the M403 or M197 numeral pairs that the M77 tablets show.
- Atlas 6.10, the powered negative for tablets carrying counts, is unaffected.
- **Reopen with:** newly published tablets carrying numerals (CISI vol. 3, new excavations). The prediction and both pair lists are frozen in the prereg.
