Project tests 12

Established results

  • “Six” followed by “fish” is a set phraseSolid

    The six-stroke sign and the fish sign appear together far more than chance, like “New York”. A popular idea that “seven + fish” means “seven stars” has almost no support.

    On the findings page · experiments/language/
