M103

Looks like: three short strokes, two over one

Adapted from the field-cady sign descriptions (Parpola 139). This describes the picture, not the meaning: a sign that looks like a fish may stand for a sound or another word.

Wells W003, W013 · Unclassified

Appears in 13 texts (30 uses), mostly on seals at Harappa and Mohenjo-daro; found at the start, middle and end of texts. In the larger ICIT corpus its Wells equivalent W003 is in 155 texts, including at Dholavira.
