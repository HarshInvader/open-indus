M391

Looks like: oval with six spokes, like a spoked wheel

Adapted from the field-cady sign descriptions (Parpola 378). This describes the picture, not the meaning: a sign that looks like a fish may stand for a sound or another word.

Wells W820 · Opener

Appears in 168 texts (195 uses), mostly on seals at Mohenjo-daro; usually starts a text. In the larger ICIT corpus its Wells equivalent W820 is in 210 texts, including at Dholavira.
