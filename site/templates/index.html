01The script

The writing of a vast early civilisation is still unread

About four thousand short texts survive from the Indus cities, 2600 to 1900 BCE, most on stone seals.

The cities rivalled those of Egypt and Mesopotamia in size and planning, yet left nothing we can read.

So we do not know their language, what they called their cities, or who ruled them. The script is the only direct evidence.

03Found so far

What we have found so far

  • There are rules. The signs appear in a fixed order, the way words do in a sentence. So these are not random marks.
  • Some seals don't follow them. A few round seals, mostly found around the Persian Gulf, break the usual order. They might be written in a foreign language.
  • No one has cracked it. Many people have claimed to read the script. We tested five of those claims, and none did better than guessing.

04How we work

How we try to read it

Nobody can read these signs yet. We look for patterns, make a guess about what the signs mean, and check it on texts we kept hidden, so we cannot fool ourselves.
