What our tests can and cannot see

  • Fitting a dictionary can count against a key that covers every sign at Indus text length. It can never count for one: keys fitted to random sign strings score higher than the true Linear B key (lb_shape_ladder).
  • A sign tied to a seal's animal is visible only if it covers an extra 20% or more of the non-unicorn seals (shorthorn bull: 20%; elephant: 40%). Weaker ties can be neither confirmed nor ruled out (animal_tie_power).
  • Tablets quoting seals from their own city cannot be seen at Indus text size: a real case from Ur III was caught in only 31% of trials (tablet_quoting).
