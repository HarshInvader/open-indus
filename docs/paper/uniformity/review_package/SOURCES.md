## Derived tables of our own that are included

These are tracked in the project repository and read by the code. They hold catalogue numbers, counts or links, not third-party text, except `pc_lines.json`, which is a sign-by-sign transcription of proto-cuneiform texts derived from CDLI.

- `experiments/audit_followups/out/b_editor_agreement.json`: links between M77 and ICIT records through the 1930s excavation registers.
- `experiments/consensus_corpus/out/seq_links.json`: best same-site ICIT record for each M77 text.
- `experiments/old_registers/registers.csv`: seal numbers and findspots from the 1931–1940 excavation reports (public domain in India).
- `experiments/reg_harappa/findspots.csv`: Harappa seal findspots (mound) from Vats 1940.
- `experiments/numeral_grammar/inventory.csv`, `experiments/signlist_robustness/variants.json`: sign-list variant partitions used for the M93 and sign-list checks.
- `experiments/protocuneiform/pc_lines.json`: proto-cuneiform lines, used only by the superseded benchmark pool `pc`.
