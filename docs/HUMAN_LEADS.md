## Potter's marks (experiments/potters_marks)
- Kenoyer & Meadow Harappa marked-sherd database (1,218 sherds with vessel forms): would allow a vessel-type test for jar sign M342's early ancestors. Others: Durrani et al. 1995 (Rehman Dheri), Franke-Vogt 2001 (Balakot), Lal et al. 2003 MASI 98 (Kalibangan), Quivron 1997 (Nausharo), Shinde 2011 (Farmana), Dani 1970-71 (Gumla).
- Hi-res photo of K-69 (Kalibangan) or H-14 (Harappa): is W056 / 'M121 M121' one ~24-stroke sign? (experiments/numeral_grammar)
- CISI vol. 3.1 data list pp. 423-441 (HARP lot numbers for Harappa inscribed objects): lets tablet-archive and chronology tests run at true lot level.
- Magnitude test (experiments/magnitude/RESULTS.md has the list): masses of Harappa inscribed tools and present-day masses of Mohenjo-daro/Chanhu-daro tools (museum records); per-bangle inner diameters (Kenoyer's stoneware bangle table, harappa.com in a normal browser); whole-vessel sizes (HARP sherd DB); more pairs of rods sharing a text.
- Sealing function (experiments/sealing_function/RESULTS.md): impressions on Lothal L-152/154/155/157 (pegs), L-150/158/209 (lockers); second observer for back types from Rao's plates; reverse descriptions for Kalibangan, Dholavira, Banawali, Kanmer, Shikarpur, Gola Dhoro.
- Assemblage test: craft evidence per Harappa Mound E/ET trench (Meadow & Kenoyer reports, Miller 1999, CISI 3.1 lot list); Lothal bead/copper workshop loci (Rao); Mohenjo-daro room-level findspots (Marshall vol. I, Mackay plate lists); Dholavira workshop areas.
- Carver hands: rescans of CISI 2:1 prints at 1200 dpi (or museum macro/RTI, >=30 px/mm), known seal-sealing pairs, ~1,000 unicorn seals (CISI vol. 3).
